# Section 102.2: Standard CMS 3:1 Age Rating Curves

## 1. Federal Age Curve Structure
In accordance with CMS guidelines under the Public Health Service (PHS) Act:
- Premium rates for adult enrollees (ages 21 through 64) cannot exceed a 3:1 ratio between the oldest adult and the youngest adult.
- Age 21 serves as the baseline benchmark index with a rating factor equal to exactly 1.000.
- Adult ages 21 to 64 scale along a monotonic curve from 1.000 to 3.000 (maximum factor at age 64+).
- Child enrollees (under age 21) are indexed at a discounted factor of 0.635 to 0.765 depending on specific year brackets.

## 2. Actuarial Rate Computation Rule
The baseline annual premium for any applicant is determined by:
Base Premium = State Benchmark Qualified Health Plan Rate * 12 * Federal Age Factor(Applicant Age).
No underwriter may inflate the age-curve coefficient beyond the established 3.000 ceiling.
