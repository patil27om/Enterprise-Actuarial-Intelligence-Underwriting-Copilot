# Section 106.1: Family Tiering & Dependent Calculation

## 1. Child Dependent Rules
Under federal and internal underwriting provisions:
- Coverage applies to biological, adopted, or foster children under age 26.
- A flat dependent allowance of $750.00 per child per annum is added to the composite family premium.
- Under ACA single-contract rules, the rating calculation only sums charges for up to the three oldest eligible children under age 21. Any additional child dependents beyond the third are covered at zero incremental cost.
