# Section 104.3: Clinical Risk Assessment & Metabolic Loadings

## 1. Body Mass Index (BMI) Tiers
Under commercial supplemental underwriting schedules:
- Normal Range: BMI 18.5 to 24.9 (Zero risk surcharge).
- Overweight: BMI 25.0 to 29.9 (Baseline review; standard rate maintained).
- Class I Obesity: BMI 30.0 to 34.9 (Incurs an actuarial risk surcharge of $180 per unit over 25.0).
- Class II & III Obesity: BMI 35.0+ (Subject to mandatory metabolic review and heightened clinical hazard rating).

## 2. Blood Pressure Guidelines
- Systolic resting blood pressure below 120 mmHg: Normal / Non-surcharge.
- Systolic resting blood pressure 120-139 mmHg: Pre-hypertension.
- Systolic resting blood pressure 140+ mmHg: Stage 2 Hypertension, requiring concurrent cardiovascular review.

## 3. Wellness Mitigation Plan
Applicants classified with Class I or Class II obesity can have their metabolic surcharge reassessed after 6 consecutive months of documented participation in a certified digital health or clinical nutrition tracking program.
