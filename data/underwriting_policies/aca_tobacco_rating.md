# Section 102.1: Federal ACA Tobacco Use Rating & Surcharges

## 1. Statutory Definition of Tobacco Use
Under Title 45 CFR § 147.102(a)(1)(iv), tobacco use is defined as the regular use of tobacco products, including cigarettes, cigars, chewing tobacco, or e-cigarettes, four or more times per week within the preceding 6 months.

## 2. Permissible Surcharge Limits
Federal regulation establishes an upper ceiling on tobacco rating:
- The maximum permissible rating factor for tobacco users is 1.5:1 (a 50% premium surcharge relative to a non-tobacco user of the same age and geographic rating area).
- In our proprietary underwriting schedules, standard tobacco usage incurs a 45% surcharge on the base age-adjusted premium.

## 3. Cessation Exemption & Retroactive Credit
Under federal wellness rules:
- Insurers must offer an approved tobacco cessation program (e.g., counseling, nicotine replacement therapy).
- If an insured individual provides proof of enrollment or completion of a recognized tobacco cessation program within 90 days of policy inception, the full tobacco surcharge must be waived, reducing their ongoing premium rate to standard non-smoker tier.
